## What PKSF is
PKSF, the Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation, is an apex development organisation set up by the Government of Bangladesh in 1990. It is a not-for-profit company, governed by a General Body and a Governing Body, and it works to reduce poverty through financing and skills training.

PKSF is second-tier. It does not lend to people directly. It funds Partner Organisations (POs), mostly NGO microfinance institutions, which lend to members organised in groups. More than 90% of borrowers are women.

## Its credit programmes
PKSF's core programmes include Jagoron for general rural and urban microcredit, Agrosor for micro-enterprise, Buniad for the ultra-poor, and Sufolon for seasonal and agricultural loans. Projects are co-funded with development partners.
