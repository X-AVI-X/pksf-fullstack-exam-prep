# Does PKSF pay salaries on time?

Checked 2026-11-16 with English and Bengali web searches.

## What was found

- **No reports of late or unpaid salaries at PKSF.** Searches for salary delays, arrears ("বেতন বকেয়া"), unpaid salaries and staff protests returned nothing about PKSF. Similar searches readily surface such stories for other public bodies in the region, so the silence means something.
- **No public employee reviews worth relying on.** Glassdoor has only a few old, anonymous salary reports (Assistant Manager about BDT 51k–90k a month, roughly five years old). There is nothing on timeliness.
- **The organisation is financially strong.** Audited accounts show large yearly surpluses (for example about BDT 2.8 billion in 2015 and 4.0 billion in 2014). The ADB says retained surplus now makes up most of PKSF's capital. PKSF earns its own income from lending to partner organisations, so salaries don't depend on yearly budget releases.
- **The benefits are formal and policy-based:** a PKSF pay structure (2018), festival and Bangla New Year allowances, provident fund, gratuity and group insurance. HR policies are described as well developed.

## Caveat that matters for this post

Check your offer letter for the **funding source of the position**:
- If it is under PKSF's **core establishment**, it is paid from PKSF's own revenue.
- If it is a **project post** funded by the World Bank, ADB or IFAD, it is often a contract renewed yearly. Pay usually comes on time, but the job can end when the project ends. Recent PKSF project posts say "initially 1 year, renewable on performance".

The Software Engineer (Full-stack) circular gives a *consolidated* BDT 1,20,000 a month. A consolidated salary often signals a contract post rather than a permanent pay-scale post. Confirm this, and whether provident fund, gratuity and pension apply, at the viva or offer stage.

## Your post specifically: Software Engineer (Full-stack), from ToR.pdf

| ToR term | What it means for pay |
|---|---|
| Works in PKSF's own **ITSD** department, at PKSF Bhaban, Dhaka; no donor project is named | Paid from PKSF's own budget, which has large yearly surpluses. Low risk of late pay. |
| **Initially 4 years, subject to annual renewal** based on performance | A contract post, not permanent. Pay should be regular, but you must be renewed every year. |
| **Consolidated BDT 1,20,000/month, inclusive of all taxes** | Tax is deducted from this amount, so take-home pay is lower (roughly BDT 1.05–1.1 lakh, depending on your tax). |
| Benefits: annual increments, festival and Bangla Noboborsho allowances, mobile allowance, group insurance | **No provident fund, gratuity or pension listed**, unlike permanent PKSF posts. |

**Verdict for this post:** salary should arrive on time, because it comes from PKSF's own funds and nothing found online suggests otherwise. The real risks are yearly renewal and the lack of retirement benefits, not late payment.

## How to verify directly

1. Ask current or former PKSF IT staff on LinkedIn: "Is salary paid by the 1st working day? Any delays during project gaps?"
2. At the viva, ask whether the post is permanent or contractual, and what the funding source is.
3. Read the appointment letter's terms on renewal, notice period and benefits.
